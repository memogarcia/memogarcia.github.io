Every couple of weeks a letter arrives from the ward office, the tax bureau, the pension service, or the vet. In Japanese. My wife and I look at it, try to figure out if it's urgent, put it on the kitchen counter, and forget about it until something breaks.

We missed a dog vaccine deadline once. Almost missed a residence tax payment. During the pregnancy there was a stretch where we were getting two or three forms a week and had no idea which ones mattered.

I built an iOS app called Nomi. You take a photo of the document and it gives you a summary in English, a list of what to do, and the dates. It runs locally on your phone, no account, no cloud. You can plug in your own API key if you want better results but the default is offline.

It's in TestFlight. I wish I had it two years ago.
