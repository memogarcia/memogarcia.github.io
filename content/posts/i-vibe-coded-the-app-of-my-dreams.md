As I get older, I find myself visiting doctors and clinics more often. The problem is that every visit leaves me with another piece of paper, or prescription, and none of these records are connected.

I wanted a single place to track my health. Most health apps ask for subscriptions, upload personal data to the cloud, and show everything in boring lists. I wanted something more intuitive: an interactive 3D model of the human body. When I get a blood test or medical report, I can just tap the organ, like the brain or liver, and attach the results. Below that, I can track daily habits like fasting and medications.

Privacy was a priority. The app runs completely on the device and uses local AI models to read and organize test results. Nothing leaves the phone unless I explicitly configure an external model.
