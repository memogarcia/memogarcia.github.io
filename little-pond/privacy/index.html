Privacy policy

Effective September 7, 2026

Little Pond does not collect or transmit personal data.

Data stored on your iPhone

Little Pond stores your pond layout, collection, discoveries, visitor memories, care progress, preferences, and unfinished changes on your device. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, cloud service, or cross-app tracking.

Little Pond does not request your location, contacts, or microphone. Camera access is optional and used only to take a picture for your pond. Apple’s photo picker shares only images you choose; the app does not request access to your full library. Pictures and detected-object stickers are processed and saved locally, without sending them to a server. Exported pond files include pictures you have added.

Exporting your pond

You can create a copy of your pond from Settings. The app creates the file only when you request it and presents Apple’s share sheet. Files you save or send are handled by the destination you choose. Little Pond does not receive a copy. The export is a data copy; Little Pond cannot import it to restore a pond.

Deleting your data

Removing Little Pond deletes its local app data, subject to Apple’s device and backup behavior. There is no remote account or cloud copy for us to delete.
