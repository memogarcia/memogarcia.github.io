Little Pond support

A quiet garden should not need a manual. These notes cover the parts that might.

Make yourself at home

Drag any plant, decoration, house, tree, or friend to move it. Tap open water to move Moss. Touch and hold an object for Rotate and Remove; removing it returns it to your collection. Undo reverses up to 20 edits in the current pond.

Collection holds objects you can place and a catalog of new garden favorites. Journal records plants and friends you have found. Movement buttons are available in Settings, with VoiceOver, and at accessibility text sizes.

Visit another pond

Tap Ponds to see nine places. Choose a place, then start a pond or move Moss there. Each place keeps its own layout.

Add a picture

Photo opens the camera. Tap Photos in the camera to choose an existing image. Little Pond lifts a detected object into a sticker and places it in the pond automatically. If no object is detected, the picture is added as-is. Pictures stay on this iPhone.

Sound and comfort

Settings has a Music switch, separate music, ambience, and effects levels, calmer motion, haptics, and object labels. Music can play in Silent Mode. If music is silent, check the Music switch, iPhone media volume, and Respect other audio setting.

Saving and export

Committed changes save on this iPhone. Settings > Export pond data creates a JSON copy and opens Apple’s share sheet. This is a data copy, not an importable backup. Little Pond has no account or cloud recovery; reinstalling can remove local progress.

iPhone widgets

Today at the Pond shows the date, Quiet Moment offers gentle reminders, and Pocket Pond is an illustrated garden shortcut. They work offline and open Little Pond when tapped.

Contact

Include the app version from Settings > About Little Pond, your iPhone model, iOS version, the action that failed, and the exact error text. Share only relevant screenshots or exported records.
