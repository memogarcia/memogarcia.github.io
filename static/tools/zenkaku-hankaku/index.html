What this converter normalizes (全角・半角変換)

Everything runs locally in your browser with no server uploads. Switch presets between To half-width, To full-width, and Japanese standard, or open More options to set rules per character group.

Alphanumerics, symbols & spaces
Convert full-width ASCII letters (Ａ-Ｚ), digits (０-９), symbols (＠－／), and ideographic spaces (　) to standard half-width characters or back, plus optional normalization for wave dash and minus variants (〜, −, ‐).
Katakana & Japanese punctuation
Merge or split voiced katakana dakuten and handakuten marks cleanly (such as ｶﾞ ⇄ ガ and ﾊﾟ ⇄ パ) along with Japanese punctuation (｡｢｣､･ ⇄ 。「」、・), while keeping hiragana and kanji untouched.
Width inspection & byte metrics
Inspect remaining full-width ASCII, full-width spaces, or half-width katakana visually and track character count, display width units (w), and UTF-8 byte size live.
