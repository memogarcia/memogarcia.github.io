    Supported breathing patterns

    The pen's height is your lung volume. Breathe in while it climbs, hold while it runs flat, breathe out while it falls. The dotted line ahead of the pen is the rest of the pattern, so you can see how long the next hold lasts before you get there. Pick a timed session (2, 5, 10, or a custom 1 to 60 minutes) or leave it open.

    Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)
    Balanced 16-second cycles: 4s inhale, 4s hold, 4s exhale, 4s hold. Press 1 to select.
    4-7-8 Breathing
    19-second calming cadence: 4s inhale, 7s hold, 8s exhale. Press 2 to select.
    Coherent Breathing (5-5)
    Smooth 10-second rhythm (six breaths per minute) with equal 5s inhale and 5s exhale phases. Press 3 to select.
    Physiological Sigh (2+1-6)
    2s inhale plus 1s top-up inhale, followed by a 6s exhale to help settle stress. Press 4 to select.
