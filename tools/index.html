Tools

  • No. 01 Focus timer · 集中 Pomodoro Wind the ring and let the pin’s shadow walk back to zero. A bell rings when it gets there.
  • No. 02 Breath pacer · 呼吸 Breathing A pen draws your breath on scrolling chart paper. Follow the line up, hold at the top, let it fall.
  • No. 03 Silent sitting · 瞑想 Meditation Light a stick of incense and sit until it burns out. A bowl rings when the smoke stops.
  • No. 04 Metabolic timer · 断食 Fasting Pour a glass when you stop eating. It is empty at your goal, and the lines on it say what your body is doing on the way.
  • No. 05 Japanese text · 全角・半角 Zenkaku ⇄ Hankaku For when ＡＢＣ should have been ABC. Full-width and half-width text, katakana included.
  • No. 06 Writing · 筆記 Typewriter One sheet and a carriage that won’t let you paste. Every letter has to be struck.
  • No. 07 Free video editor · 編集 Cut Trim clips, add captions and export video in your browser. No uploads or sign-up required.
